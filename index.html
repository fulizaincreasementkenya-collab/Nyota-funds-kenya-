Empowerment Grants 2026

Non-repayable financial support for Youth, Women, and SME Development across all 47 Counties.

Program Terms & Agreement

1. Eligibility: Applicants must be Kenyan citizens aged 18 and above holding a valid National ID card.


2. Usage of Funds: Grants are non-repayable and intended specifically for business establishment, farming expansion, education support, or personal economic development.


3. Processing & Activation: All details provided must match government registry records. A standard, non-refundable activation fee applies to process M-PESA STK disbursements.


4. One Grant Per Citizen: Beneficiaries are allowed one disbursement request per calendar year.
